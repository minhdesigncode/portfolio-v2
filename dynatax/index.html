Sep 7, 2026 · Case study

Untangling settings for a vibe-coded tax platform

How we turned DynaTax’s fragmented settings into two clear homes, one for the person and one for the business, on a US platform that connects business owners with tax pros.

Client
DynaTax AI, US TaxTech
Role
Product Designer (Freelance)
Team
With the reLab product design team
Epic
Settings: Account, Business, Billing, Onboarding
01 · Overview

One platform, two kinds of users

DynaTax helps US businesses run their bookkeeping and tax work. It serves two account types: business owners (truckers, nail salons, freelancers, small businesses) and tax pros, the freelance CPAs who prepare their taxes. An internal community connects the two, so owners can find help and tax pros can find clients.

Business owner accountsTax Pro accountsInternal communityAI bookkeeping
02 · Challenge

We inherited a vibe-coded product

DynaTax’s dev team had built the whole platform with AI, from the founder’s ideas and AI-generated docs. It worked, but it wasn’t designed. Our job was to revamp it into a web product that actually holds up.

Structure

Two levels, one page

Personal account settings and organization settings were mixed together in one tab bar, though they belong to different levels.

Scale

Many businesses per person

One owner might run an LLC, a trucking business and a 1099 side gig, each with its own plan and data.

Fit

One layout for everyone

A trucker and a nail salon owner track very different things, but saw the same product.

03 · Research

Learning from TaxDome, Xero and tax communities

Since our epic was Settings, we focused there. We studied TaxDome, Xero and QuickBooks, plus tax communities on Reddit, to see how mature products separate the person from the business.

Xero was the closest match: it keeps organization settings and user settings at clearly different levels. It became our main reference.

04 · Information architecture

Split the person from the business

Once the IA was agreed, we gave each level its own entry point, so users always know whether a change affects them or their whole business.

Avatar · bottom left

Account settings

About you, across every business you belong to.

  • Personal information
  • Business management (your organizations)
  • Billing & invoices across businesses
  • Account verification
  • Account security
  • Notification setup
  • Community alias and profile
Business switcher · top right

Business settings

About one organization, shared by its team.

  • Organization overview: name, logo, community profile
  • Industry setting
  • Pricing plan & subscription
  • Usage meters, payment methods, invoices
  • Team members & roles
  • Integrations
05 · Onboarding

A layout for each kind of business

During onboarding, owners answer a short survey that places them in a segment: trucker, nail salon, freelancer, small business. The industry they pick unlocks the right tools, like per diem and IFTA for truckers or quarterly estimates for 1099 contractors. Owners can change it later in Business settings.

06 · Flows & wireframes

Organizations, end to end

Every path from an organization card: manage plan, compare plans, view usage and invoices, or create a new organization.

07 · Account settings

Everything about you, from the avatar

One menu, status at a glance

The avatar menu shows how many businesses you run and whether you are verified before you open a page.

  • Business count under Business Management
  • “Not verified” under Verification

Personal information

Each field is its own row with its own Edit. Verification sits next to the data it checks.

  • Inline “Verify now” for email and phone
  • Community alias sets how you appear in the community
  • Tax info: SSN, filing status, dependents

Verify in place

A six-digit code confirms email or phone without leaving the page.

  • Resend with a 30s cooldown
  • One clear Verify action

Business management

Every business you belong to, with your role and its plan. Problems show inline, each with one fix.

  • Plan limit warning → Upgrade Plan
  • Missing entity → Setup Entity
  • Set default or remove

All billing

One table for every subscription you pay for, across all your businesses.

  • Filter by plan and payment status
  • View detail, change plan, cancel

Safe logout

Logging out during a bank sync or AI report could lose data, so waiting is the default.

  • Primary: Wait & Close
  • Secondary: Log out anyway
08 · Business settings

Everything about one organization, from the top right

Plan, usage and payments

One page answers the questions owners ask most: what am I paying, how much have I used, and how do I pay.

  • Current plan with features and Upgrade
  • Usage meters that warn before limits hit
  • Payment methods and invoice history
09 · Design system

Built on DynaTax UI Kit 2.0

Every screen uses the shared UI Kit we maintained with reLab: global color tokens, an Inter type scale, and components like buttons and metric cards with full state coverage.

10 · Impact

What changed

Two clear homes

Account and business settings now live at different entry points, so users know what a change affects.

Fewer blind spots

Plan limits, missing entity data and verification surface before they block tax work.

Ready to scale

Industry segments and shared components let new business types plug in without a redesign.
