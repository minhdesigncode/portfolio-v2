Sep 7, 2026 · Case study

Settings that scale with every business you run

Account Settings, Business Management and Billing for DynaTax AI, an AI bookkeeping and tax workspace for small businesses and tax pros in the US.

Client
DynaTax AI
Role
Product Designer (Freelance)
Team
With Relab product design team
Scope
Settings, Organizations, Billing, Verification
01 · Overview

One login, many businesses

DynaTax AI helps small business owners keep IRS-ready books and connect with verified tax pros. As users started running several businesses under one account, settings had to handle identity, entities, plans and billing without getting in the way of tax work.

Account SettingsBusiness ManagementBilling & InvoicesVerificationNotificationsDocuments IA
02 · Challenge

Where users got stuck

Multi-entity

Too many businesses

Owners with an LLC, a 1099 side business and a trucking company had no clear way to switch, compare or set a default.

Billing

Hidden plan limits

Each business has its own plan. Users hit Free plan limits without warning, mid tax season.

Compliance

Incomplete entity data

Missing entity details or an unverified identity quietly blocked tax filing and deadline tracking.

03 · Research

Benchmarking TaxDome

I mapped TaxDome's settings structure against what DynaTax users actually need, then set a goal for each settings group before drawing any screen.

04 · Information architecture

Eight groups, one clear home

Profile, Organizations, Security, Notification, Verification, Business Profile, Billing and Community. Every setting sits two clicks away from the avatar menu.

05 · Flows & wireframes

Organizations, end to end

Every path from an organization card: manage plan, compare plans, view usage and invoices, or create a new organization.

06 · Final design

Key screens

Settings from the avatar

A slide-out account menu shows status at a glance before the user opens a page.

  • Business count under Business Management
  • "Not verified" under Verification

Business Management

Each business is a card with role, owner, members and industry. Problems show up inline, each with one fix.

  • Plan limit warning → Upgrade Plan
  • Missing entity → Setup Entity
  • Filter by role, plan, status

Personal Information

Verification sits next to the data it checks, so users verify email and phone in place.

  • Inline "Verify now" per field
  • Tax info: SSN, filing status, dependents

Safe logout

Logging out during a bank sync or AI report could lose data, so waiting is the default.

  • Primary: Wait & Close
  • Secondary: Log out anyway
07 · Impact

What changed

Faster setup

Creating businesses, setting up entities and choosing plans got simpler for multi-business owners.

Fewer blind spots

Plan limits, missing entity data and verification now surface before they block tax work.

Reusable patterns

Status cards, inline warnings and settings rows joined the shared design system with Relab.
