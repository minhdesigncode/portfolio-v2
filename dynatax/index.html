01 — Overview

One login, many businesses

DynaTax AI helps small business owners keep IRS-ready books and connect with verified tax pros. As users started running several businesses under one account, the settings area had to handle identity, entities, plans and billing without getting in the way of tax work.

Account Settings Business Management Billing & Invoices Account Verification Notification Setup Documents IA
02 — Challenge

Where users got stuck

Multi-entity

Too many businesses

Owners with an LLC, a 1099 side business and a trucking company had no clear way to switch, compare or set a default.

Billing

Hidden plan limits

Each business has its own plan. Users hit Free plan limits without warning, mid tax season.

Compliance

Incomplete entity data

Missing entity details or an unverified identity quietly blocked tax filing and deadline tracking.

06 — Final Design

Key screens

Settings from the avatar

A slide-out account menu shows status at a glance before the user even opens a page.

  • Business count under Business Management
  • "Not verified" status under Verification

Business Management

Each business is a card with role, owner, members and industry. Problems show up inline, each with one fix.

  • Plan limit warning → Upgrade Plan
  • Missing entity → Setup Entity
  • Filter by role, subscription, status

Personal Information

Verification lives next to the data it checks. Users verify email and phone in place instead of hunting for a separate flow.

  • Inline "Verify now" per field
  • Tax info summary: SSN, filing status, dependents

Safe logout

Logging out during a bank sync or AI report could lose data. A guard dialog makes waiting the default.

  • Primary action: Wait & Close
  • Secondary: Log out anyway
07 — Impact

What changed

Faster setup

Streamlined creating businesses, setting up entities and choosing plans for multi-business owners.

Fewer blind spots

Plan limits, missing entity data and verification now surface before they block tax work.

Reusable patterns

Status cards, inline warnings and settings list rows were added to the shared design system with Relab.
