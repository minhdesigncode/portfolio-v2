Sep 7, 2026 · Case study

Untangling settings for a vibe-coded tax platform

How we turned DynaTax's fragmented settings into two clear levels, Account and Business, for a US platform that connects small business owners with freelance tax pros.

Client
DynaTax AI, US TaxTech
Role
Product Designer (Freelance)
Team
With reLab product design team
Epic
Settings: Account, Business, Billing, Onboarding
01 · Overview

Bookkeeping, tax work and a community in one place

DynaTax manages accounting and tax work for small businesses in the US. It is also a community: a place where business owners, from truckers to nail salon owners and freelancers, meet freelance CPAs and tax pros who can help them.

Account type 1

Business owner

Runs one or more businesses, keeps books IRS-ready, finds a tax pro when needed.

Account type 2

Tax pro

Freelance CPA or tax professional who manages clients and builds a reputation in the community.

02 · Challenge

We inherited a fully vibe-coded product

The DynaTax dev team had built the platform with AI, from the founder's idea and AI-generated docs. It worked, but it wasn't designed. Our job was to revamp it into a web product that is actually easy to use. Our epic was Settings.

Settings mixed two different levels: the person and the business. Organization settings and account settings sat side by side in one tab bar.

Fragmented

Two levels, one page

Profile, Organizations, Billing and Community shared one tab bar, so it was unclear what applied to you and what applied to your business.

Multi-entity

Many businesses, no home

Owners with an LLC, a 1099 side business and a trucking company had no clear place to switch, compare or set a default.

Compliance

Hidden blockers

Plan limits, missing entity details and unverified identity quietly blocked tax filing.

03 · Research

Learning from TaxDome, Xero and QuickBooks

We studied tax and accounting platforms (TaxDome, Xero, QuickBooks) and tax communities such as Reddit's tax forums. Xero became our main reference: it is very close to DynaTax and handles the split between organization settings and account settings especially well.

04 · Information architecture

Two levels, two entry points

Once the IA was agreed, we split settings in two. Anything about you lives under your avatar at the bottom left. Anything about the business lives at the top right, next to the business switcher.

Avatar · bottom left

Account settings

About the person

  • Personal information · name, contact, address, tax info
  • Business management · all your businesses
  • Billing & invoices · across businesses
  • Account verification · email, phone, identity
  • Account security · password, 2FA
  • Notification setup · SMS, digest, quiet hours
  • Community alias · how you appear
Business switcher · top right

Business settings

About one business

  • Organization overview · name, logo, community profile
  • Industry · trucker, small business, freelancer
  • Pricing plan & subscription
  • Usage meters · AI queries, documents, storage
  • Payment methods & invoices
  • Team members & roles
05 · Onboarding & industry

Different businesses, different layouts

During registration, a short survey asks what kind of business you run, such as trucking, a nail salon or freelance work. The answer sets the industry, which decides the features and layout you get: per diem and IFTA for truckers, invoicing for small businesses, quarterly estimates for 1099 contractors.

The same flow, wireframed end to end for organizations: manage plan, compare plans, view usage and invoices, or create a new one.

06 · Account settings

Everything about you, one click from your avatar

Account menu

A slide-out menu shows status at a glance before you open a page.

  • Business count under Business Management
  • "Not verified" under Verification

Personal information

Verification sits next to the data it checks, and the community alias is set right here.

  • Inline "Verify now" per field
  • Tax info: SSN, filing status, dependents

Verify in place

A 6-digit code confirms email or phone without leaving the page.

  • Resend with a 30s timer
  • One clear primary action

Business management

Every business you own or belong to, with role, owner, members and industry. Problems show inline, each with one fix.

  • Plan limit → Upgrade Plan
  • Missing entity → Setup Entity
  • Set a default business

All billing

One table for every subscription across your businesses.

  • Status chips: Due soon, Processing, Paid
  • View detail, change plan, cancel

Safe logout

Logging out mid bank sync or AI report could lose data, so waiting is the default.

  • Primary: Wait & Close
  • Secondary: Log out anyway
07 · Business settings

Plans, usage and payments for each business

Pricing plan & usage

The current plan, what it includes and how much is left, in one view. Meters turn yellow and red before limits are hit.

  • Usage meters reset monthly
  • Payment methods per business
  • Invoice history with export
08 · Design system

Built on DynaTax UI Kit 2.0

Every screen uses the shared UI Kit we maintained with reLab: global color tokens, an Inter type scale, button states and data components like metric cards.

09 · Impact

What changed

Clear mental model

"Me" settings under the avatar, "my business" settings at the top right. No more guessing which level a setting belongs to.

Fewer blind spots

Plan limits, missing entity data and verification surface before they block tax work.

Ready to scale

Industry-based layouts and reusable components let DynaTax add new segments without redesigning settings.
