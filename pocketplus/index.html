Pocketplus+: your smart wallet, made for Vietnamese people

01 · The moment

Imagine this

You're at the checkout. The cashier has scanned your items and you reach for your wallet. But your card… it's gone. Panic sets in.

Or you're at the airport, the check-in line is long, and you reach for your physical boarding pass. Your heart skips a beat… it's not there.

These aren't minor inconveniences. Pocketplus+ is designed to free you from moments like these.

02 · Overview

One pocket for everything you carry

Pocketplus+ is a wallet app for storing and managing personal cards and important documents on the phone, built for urban Vietnamese users. A simple design and strong security bring convenience, speed and peace of mind, anytime and anywhere.

Our goal: design an intuitive app that lets people manage their digital assets easily and smartly, removing the need for a physical wallet and the risk of losing or damaging it.

04 · Competitor analysis

What Apple and Google already do well

Apple Wallet

  • Stores credit cards, boarding passes, event tickets and membership cards.
  • Popular in high iPhone-share markets: the US, Canada, Japan, Australia and parts of Europe.
  • High security with Face ID or Touch ID.

Google Wallet

  • Stores credit and debit cards, gift cards and event tickets.
  • Favored in Android-heavy markets across Asia, South America and Europe.
  • Security with fingerprint authentication and PIN.

What if there was a smart wallet designed for Vietnamese people, as convenient as Apple and Google Wallet, but focused on local needs, security and a wider range of private and public uses?

06 · Problem statement

The real problem

People who often forget their ID, membership cards, tickets or bank details need one central, easy-to-reach place for that information, because keeping documents in general apps or cluttered photo libraries makes them hard to find, which leads to frustration and wasted time.

11 · Feedback

What users told us

  • Logo. The "add card" icon in the logo felt similar to Timo's.
  • Bank card flow. The UX of adding a bank card works well.
  • ID card flow. Two screens had an unclear purpose. If they show card details, move them to a separate details flow.
  • Empty cards. A card without bank details yet should look blank.
  • Card details. Tapping a card should open details with copy actions.
  • Security. Hide transaction history behind an extra step, in case the card is shown to someone else.
