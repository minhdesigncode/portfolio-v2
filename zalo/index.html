Sep 2, 2026 · Case study

What if Zalo Chat became our neighborhood's trusted community marketplace?

A 3-month UX project supported by Mirr Design: researching how apartment residents in Vietnam buy, sell and exchange in Zalo groups, then designing and testing a better way to do it inside the chat.

Timeline
Oct 2024 to Jan 2025
Role
UX/UI Designer in training
Skills
UX research, usability testing, wireframing
Team
2 designers, mentored by Mirr Design
01 · Challenge

70 million users, and a very messy group chat

With 70+ million users, Zalo is a huge opportunity for shop owners, local communities and small businesses to connect and trade. But managing community interactions is hard: people struggle with the sheer volume of daily group messages, messy photos, and no real way to follow up.

How can we change the way people in residential areas buy and sell, with a seamless peer-to-peer experience inside the Zalo groups they already trust?

03 · Research

First findings

82%of Vietnamese users named Zalo their most preferred network for messaging, calls and utilities
$5Bprojected value of Vietnam's secondhand market in 2026, up from $1.1B today

Research goals

  • Understand user behavior and pain points
  • Evaluate existing features and experience
  • Define requirements for new features

Research focus

  • People who have bought or sold in Zalo or Facebook groups, or other P2P channels
  • 50+ interview questions across platforms
  • 8 in-depth user interviews
04 · Insights

What we heard

Young professionals25 to 35
Busy parents30 to 45
Small business owners25 to 50
  1. Zalo groups are mainly used for building updates and announcements.
  2. Users find it hard to locate what they need in the chat.
  3. Trust in product quality and sellers is low.
  4. The current interface isn't built for buying and selling.
  5. There is strong potential for Zalo to become an internal marketplace.
  6. Users want seller ratings, transparent payment, order tracking, reviews and safe handling of personal data.
05 · Persona

Meet Thùy Nhiên

A 28-year-old office worker in a busy apartment complex who loves hidden gems and good deals. Her building's Zalo group was meant for community news, but it has become a chaotic free-for-all.

When a neighbor posted a secondhand item, she was excited, until an hour later she tried to find the post again and got lost scrolling. She wants the chat to feel less like a source of anxiety and more like a simple marketplace with her neighbors.

"I'm tired of scrolling through countless messages and messy images just to find what I need. I want a simple, reliable way to shop in my community, at a fair price, without worrying about the risks."

06 · Problems → ideas

Three problems, three directions

ProblemClunky shopping. Finding products, comparing prices and completing a deal in a group chat is inconvenient.
How might wemake product discovery, talking to sellers and paying feel seamless inside Zalo groups?
IdeaBetter seller chat, smart search, and a streamlined checkout with order tracking.
ProblemThin product info. A photo and a line of text isn't enough; people want details, reviews and proof of origin.
How might wegive buyers complete, trustworthy product information?
IdeaFull product pages with images, video, reviews and origin verification.
ProblemLow trust. Buyers worry about fakes, poor quality and scams.
How might wemake sellers and products credible and transparent?
IdeaRatings, seller verification, buyer protection and community feedback.
07 · Structure

Card sorting and user flows

  • 1. Search for products in messages or the group marketplace
  • 2a. Buy any product
  • 2b. Make a payment
  • 3. Create or view thread messages
  • 4a. View a seller's profile
  • 4b. Chat with a seller
  • 5. Review and rate a purchased order

10 · Usability testing

What testing taught us

Trust and security

  • Stronger user protection policies
  • An interface that inspires confidence when buying and selling

Product information

  • A "Call" button on product pages, like Chợ Tốt
  • Simpler, less overwhelming filters

Intuitive interface

  • A clearer icon for "Buy" than the shopping cart
  • Consistent star ratings

Clear, friendly content

  • Easy, welcoming copy
  • Clear wording on shipping responsibility
11 · Takeaways

What I learned

Research to story

Hands-on research, interviewing, testing and storytelling, with guidance from mentors at Mirr Design.

UX is the whole journey

Good UX covers everything from first discovery to the review after delivery, not just screens.

Teamwork

Working closely with a teammate sharpened how we split work and decide together.
